Privacy Policy

JunkSweep — Effective Date: June 4, 2026

All photo and video processing is done 100% on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or share any of your photos or videos.

1. Overview

JunkSweep is a photo cleaning and storage management application. Our core function is to scan your device's photo library, identify junk photos (blurry, duplicate, screenshots, overexposed, low-quality images), and help you delete them to free up storage space.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect, upload, or transmit any of your photos or videos. All image analysis and processing happens entirely on your device.

The only data that may be collected is related to in-app purchases, handled by our third-party payment provider (see Section 4).

3. Permissions We Use

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — Required to scan, analyze, display, and delete photos on your device. This permission is essential for the app's core functionality.
  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO — Required to scan, analyze, display, and delete videos on your device.

These permissions are used solely for the app's photo/video cleaning features. No media files are ever uploaded or shared.

4. Third-Party Services

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases. RevenueCat may collect:

  • Anonymous device identifiers (for purchase verification)
  • Purchase transaction records
  • App version and device OS information

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. Data Storage

  • Premium status — Stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences
  • Scan preferences — Stored locally on your device
  • No cloud storage — We do not operate any servers or databases storing user data

6. Children's Privacy

JunkSweep is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
